About.

A little about the person
behind the projects.

Hi, I’m Fabian.

I’m into building things and figuring out how they work. My background is in Computer Engineering at the University of Waterloo, and I spend a lot of my time working with FPGAs, embedded systems, and software.

I enjoy the hands-on part: trying an idea, testing it, and working through the little details until it does what I had in mind. This site is where I share some of those projects and what I’ve learned along the way.

Away from the workbench
